# Attempt record — complete before consulting the public guide

- Attempt ID/date; prior exposure to C-20/answers; practice time spent:
- Method: supplied-export review / independently decoded capture / separately executed fixture verification (choose only what you did):
- Tool/version/command and limitations, or browser-only method:
- Scope, exact files/hashes, exclusions, stop conditions:
- Inventory: BSSID | file/frame | owner assertion | observed advertisement | unresolved ownership:
- Prioritized decision log: question | why first | permitted action | evidence | decision | missing evidence:

For each claim use:

ID | bounded claim | status (SUPPORTED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / NOT_TESTED) | filename + hash + frame/field or document section | alternative explanation | impact assumption | next authorized test | owner | retest closure condition

Include an advertised-policy comparison, an ownership hypothesis that cannot become a finding, a no-finding observation with narrow scope, and a live outcome that was not tested. Distinguish the policy observation from business risk: do not manufacture a severity score from an SSID or missing inventory row.

Client summary (maximum 150 words): what was examined, what is supported, what is unknown, the first action/owner, and what must not be treated as completed remediation.

Retest proposal: same BSSID and controlled client; permitted method/window; route/AP/client collection points; expected positive/negative controls; stop conditions; owner-approved rollback and cleanup. Proposal only, not execution.

Reviewer record: reviewer/date/relationship or SELF-REVIEW; each rubric criterion; citations checked; challenge to one alternative explanation; required correction. Do not fill in human approval that did not occur.

Retry/change log: original claim | reason for correction | new evidence or reasoning | unresolved limitation. Preserve the initial attempt.

Data handoff record: originals read-only; derived files labeled; recipient/access method; redactions; owner-agreed retention date and deletion/backup procedure. Do not upload real captures, personal data or secrets to public issues or feedback forms.
