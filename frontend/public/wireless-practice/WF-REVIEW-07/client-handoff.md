# Client handoff template

## Executive summary
Describe the offline fictional scope, supported observation, unresolved ownership/client-policy questions and the next owner action. Avoid “network secured”, “rogue confirmed” or “retest passed” without their missing evidence.

## Evidence index
Original file/hash; frame/field; derived export/tool; custodian; collection limitation. These captures were generated, not collected; do not invent an analyst collection signature.

## Findings, non-findings and unresolved questions
Keep supported observations separate from risk hypotheses and NOT TESTED outcomes. State affected BSSID and exact observation, policy expectation, alternative, impact assumptions and remediation owner. A no-finding applies only to the checked evidence, not the whole environment.

## Retest and acceptance
CR-20 advertisement comparison is a narrow check. Proposed live closure requires approved clients/APs, negotiated AKM/PMF and rejection controls, owner inventory correlation, logs and cleanup. State no actual client test or reset occurred here.

## Delivery and retention
For this synthetic pack, keep a local attempt and source hashes. For a future real engagement, agree encrypted delivery, named recipients, access expiry, minimal collection, legal/contract retention and deletion including backups before work begins. Redact derived reports without overwriting originals. Do not send real credentials or captures through the platform feedback form. The downloadable worksheet has no server submission or remote deletion facility; local copies are the learner's responsibility.
