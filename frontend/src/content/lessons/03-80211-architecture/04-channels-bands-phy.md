- Understand HT/VHT/HE/EHT capabilities, MCS, spatial streams, QAM, OFDMA, MLO

## Theory

### 2.4 GHz Deep Dive

**Frequency:** 2.400-2.4835 GHz, 83.5 MHz total

**Channels:** 1-14, 20 MHz each, center frequencies:
- Ch1 2412 MHz, Ch2 2417, Ch3 2422, Ch4 2427, Ch5 2432, Ch6 2437, Ch7 2442, Ch8 2447, Ch9 2452, Ch10 2457, Ch11 2462, Ch12 2467, Ch13 2472, Ch14 2484 (Japan only, 11b only)

**Overlapping:** 20 MHz channel overlaps ±2 channels — Ch1 overlaps Ch2-5, Ch6 overlaps Ch2-10, Ch11 overlaps Ch7-13. Only 1,6,11 non-overlapping in US (FCC Ch1-11). In EU/ETSI Ch1-13, 1,5,9,13 non-overlapping with 20 MHz.

**40 MHz in 2.4 GHz:**
- Bonds 2× 20 MHz channels — e.g., Ch6 HT40+ uses Ch6 primary + Ch10 secondary (extension channel above), Ch6 HT40- uses Ch6 primary + Ch2 secondary (below)
- Doubles rate, but causes overlap — Ch6 HT40+ overlaps Ch1-11 almost all, causes interference, bad practice, especially in dense environments
- **For PT:** If beacon shows HT 40 MHz in 2.4 GHz, Medium finding bad practice, causes interference, should be 20 MHz only. Config `ht_capab=[HT40+][HT40-]` in 2.4 GHz is misconfig. Evidence: Beacon HT capabilities, channel, `ht_capab` from hostapd.conf.

**Propagation:** Good through walls (12.5 cm wavelength), longer range (~50m indoor, 100m outdoor 2 dBi), but more interference — microwave ovens 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth FHSS 2.4 GHz, Zigbee 2.4 GHz, all share.

**Power:** Typically 20 dBm (100 mW) EIRP max in US, 20 dBm in EU, varies by regdom, `iw reg get` shows.

**Data Rates:** 802.11b 1/2/5.5/11 Mbps DSSS, 802.11g 6-54 Mbps OFDM, 802.11n up to 150 Mbps 20 MHz 1 stream, 300 Mbps 40 MHz 2 streams, 600 Mbps 40 MHz 4 streams.

### 5 GHz Deep Dive

**Frequency:** 5.150-5.825 GHz, 675 MHz total, UNII bands:

- **UNII-1:** 36-48, 5170-5250 MHz, 200 mW, indoor, no DFS
- **UNII-2:** 52-64, 5250-5330 MHz, 200 mW, DFS, radar
- **UNII-2e:** 100-144, 5490-5730 MHz, 200 mW, DFS, radar
- **UNII-3:** 149-165, 5735-5835 MHz, 1W, outdoor, no DFS (US), 25 mW in EU

**Channels:** 25× 20 MHz, 12× 40 MHz, 6× 80 MHz, 2× 160 MHz in US

**DFS:** Dynamic Frequency Selection — AP must detect radar in UNII-2/2e and vacate channel within 10 seconds, switch to another channel. If AP on DFS channel and radar detected, clients disconnect, AP switches. For PT: Recon should monitor multiple channels, AP may change channel if radar, need to scan all.

**Propagation:** Less through walls than 2.4 GHz (6 cm wavelength), shorter range (~25m indoor, 50m outdoor), less interference (less devices in 5 GHz), higher rates, more channels, less overlapping.

**Power:** Up to 30 dBm (1W) in UNII-3, 23 dBm in UNII-1/2/2e, varies by regdom and band.

**Data Rates:** 802.11a 6-54 Mbps OFDM, 802.11n up to 600 Mbps 40 MHz 4 streams, 802.11ac up to 6.9 Gbps 160 MHz 8 streams 256-QAM, 802.11ax up to 9.6 Gbps 160 MHz 8 streams 1024-QAM OFDMA.

### 6 GHz Deep Dive (Wi-Fi 6E/7)

**Frequency:** 5.925-7.125 GHz, 1200 MHz total, U-NII bands:

- **U-NII-5:** 1-93, 5925-6425 MHz, 59× 20 MHz, low power indoor 24 dBm, standard power 36 dBm with AFC
- **U-NII-6:** 95-111, 6425-6525 MHz, 8× 20 MHz, low power indoor only 24 dBm
- **U-NII-7:** 113-173, 6525-6875 MHz, 30× 20 MHz, low power indoor 24 dBm, standard power 36 dBm with AFC
- **U-NII-8:** 175-233, 6875-7125 MHz, 29× 20 MHz, low power indoor 24 dBm, standard power 36 dBm with AFC

**Channels:** 59× 20 MHz, 29× 40 MHz, 14× 80 MHz, 7× 160 MHz, 3× 320 MHz (Wi-Fi 7)

**Only WPA3:** 6 GHz only allows WPA3 — no WPA2, no WEP, no TKIP, open must be OWE (Opportunistic Wireless Encryption) or WPA3. Security benefit: WPA3-only mandates PMF required, no transition downgrade risk if pure 6 GHz (no WPA2). If you see WPA2 on 6 GHz, High finding non-compliant.

**Propagation:** Even less through walls than 5 GHz (5 cm wavelength), shortest range (~15m indoor, 30m outdoor), cleanest spectrum (less interference, new band), highest rates, 6 GHz has less congestion.

**Power:** Low power indoor 24 dBm (250 mW), standard power 36 dBm (4W) with AFC (Automated Frequency Coordination) — AFC checks database for incumbent users (fixed satellite, etc.) and allows higher power if no incumbent.

**Data Rates:** 802.11ax up to 9.6 Gbps 160 MHz, 802.11be up to 46 Gbps 320 MHz MLO 4096-QAM.

**For PT:** 6 GHz only WPA3 — good for security, but transition mode with 2.4/5 GHz may have downgrade risk if same password. Evidence: Beacon band 6 GHz, security WPA3-only, PMF required.

### Channel Width & HT/VHT/HE/EHT

**20 MHz:** Baseline, 1× 20 MHz, good for 2.4 GHz, always supported

**40 MHz:** 2× 20 MHz bonded, doubles rate, but 2.4 GHz 40 MHz bad practice (overlaps), 5/6 GHz good

**80 MHz:** 4× 20 MHz, 5/6 GHz only, good, 802.11ac/ax

**160 MHz:** 8× 20 MHz, 5/6 GHz, very high rate, but less practical due to interference, DFS, need 8 channels contiguous

**320 MHz:** 16× 20 MHz, 6 GHz only, Wi-Fi 7, highest rate, needs 16 channels contiguous, very high rate but rare

**HT (802.11n, High Throughput):**
- HT Capabilities IE (45): HT Capabilities Info (2 bytes, LDPC, 20/40 MHz, SM Power Save, Greenfield, Short GI 20/40, TX STBC, RX STBC, HT Delayed Block Ack, Max AMSDU length, DSSS/CCK HT40, PSMP, 40 MHz Intolerant, L-SIG TXOP protection), A-MPDU Parameters (1 byte, Max A-MPDU length, Min MPDU Start Spacing), Supported MCS Set (16 bytes, RX MCS bitmask, RX Highest Supported Data Rate, TX MCS Set), HT Extended Capabilities (2 bytes), TX Beamforming Capabilities (4 bytes), ASEL Capabilities (1 byte)
- HT Operation IE (61): Primary Channel, HT Operation Info (Secondary Channel Offset, STA Channel Width, RIFS Mode, HT Protection, Non-Greenfield STAs present, OBSS Non-HT STAs present, Channel Center Frequency Segment 2, Dual Beacon, Dual CTS Protection, STBC Beacon, L-SIG TXOP Protection Full Support, PCO Active, PCO Phase), Basic MCS Set
- **For PT:** HT Capabilities show 20/40 MHz, MCS, spatial streams. If 2.4 GHz AP shows HT40, Medium finding.

**VHT (802.11ac, Very High Throughput):**
- VHT Capabilities IE (191): VHT Capabilities Info (4 bytes, Max MPDU length, Supported Channel Width Set, RX LDPC, Short GI 80/160, TX STBC, RX STBC, SU Beamformer, SU Beamformee, Compressed Steering Number of Beamformer Antennas, Number of Sounding Dimensions, MU Beamformer, MU Beamformee, VHT TXOP PS, HTC-VHT, Max A-MPDU length exponent, VHT Link Adaptation, RX Antenna Pattern Consistency, TX Antenna Pattern Consistency), Supported VHT MCS and NSS Set (8 bytes, RX MCS Map, RX Highest Supported Long GI Data Rate, TX MCS Map, TX Highest Supported Long GI Data Rate)
- VHT Operation IE (192): Channel Width (0 20/40, 1 80, 2 160, 3 80+80), Channel Center Frequency Segment 0, Segment 1, Basic VHT MCS and NSS Set
- **For PT:** VHT Capabilities show 80/160 MHz, 256-QAM, MU-MIMO. 5 GHz only.

**HE (802.11ax, High Efficiency):**
- HE Capabilities IE (255 ext 35): HE MAC Capabilities Info (6 bytes, HTC HE, TWT Requester/Responder, Trigger Frame MAC Padding Duration, Multi-TID Aggregation RX, All Ack, TRS, BSR, Broadcast TWT, 32-bit BA Bitmap, MU Cascading, Ack-Enabled Aggregation, Group Addressed Multi-STA BlockAck, etc.), HE PHY Capabilities Info (11 bytes, Channel Width Set, Puncturing, Device Class, LDPC Coding in Payload, HE SU PPDU with 1x HE-LTF and 0.8us GI, Midamble TX/RX Max NSTS, NDP with 4x HE-LTF and 3.2us GI, STBC TX/RX <=80 MHz, Doppler TX/RX, Full Bandwidth UL MU-MIMO, Partial Bandwidth UL MU-MIMO, DCM Max Constellation TX/RX, DCM Max NSS TX/RX, RX Partial BW SU in 20MHz MU, SU Beamformer/Beamformee, MU Beamformer, BSS Color, etc.), Supported HE MCS and NSS Set, PPE Thresholds
- HE Operation IE (255 ext 36): HE Operation Parameters (BSS Color, Partial BSS Color, BSS Color Disabled, etc.), BSS Color Information, Basic HE MCS and NSS Set, VHT Operation Information, Co-Hosted BSS, etc.
- **For PT:** HE Capabilities show OFDMA, 1024-QAM, BSS coloring, 20/40/80/160 MHz. 2.4/5/6 GHz.

**EHT (802.11be, Extremely High Throughput, Wi-Fi 7):**
- EHT Capabilities IE (255 ext 108): EHT MAC Capabilities, EHT PHY Capabilities, Supported EHT MCS and NSS Set, EHT PPE Thresholds, etc. — MLO, 4096-QAM, 320 MHz, 16 streams
- EHT Operation IE (255 ext 109): EHT Operation Parameters, etc.
- **For PT:** EHT Capabilities show MLO, 4096-QAM, 320 MHz. 2.4/5/6 GHz, Wi-Fi 7.

### MCS & Spatial Streams

**MCS (Modulation and Coding Scheme):** Index for modulation, coding rate, spatial streams, data rate

- MCS 0: BPSK 1/2, 6.5 Mbps 20 MHz 1 stream
- MCS 1: QPSK 1/2, 13 Mbps
- MCS 2: QPSK 3/4, 19.5 Mbps
- MCS 3: 16-QAM 1/2, 26 Mbps
- MCS 4: 16-QAM 3/4, 39 Mbps
- MCS 5: 64-QAM 2/3, 52 Mbps
- MCS 6: 64-QAM 3/4, 58.5 Mbps
- MCS 7: 64-QAM 5/6, 65 Mbps
- MCS 8: 256-QAM 3/4, 78 Mbps (802.11ac)
- MCS 9: 256-QAM 5/6, 86.7 Mbps (802.11ac)
- MCS 10: 1024-QAM 3/4, 90 Mbps (802.11ax)
- MCS 11: 1024-QAM 5/6, 108.3 Mbps (802.11ax)
- MCS 12: 4096-QAM 3/4, 120 Mbps (802.11be)
- MCS 13: 4096-QAM 5/6, 135 Mbps (802.11be)

Higher MCS = higher rate, but needs better SNR.

**Spatial Streams:** 1 stream SISO, 2 streams 2×2 MIMO, 3 streams 3×3, 4 streams 4×4, up to 8 streams 802.11ac, 16 streams 802.11be. More streams = higher rate, but need multiple antennas.

**For PT:** MCS and spatial streams shown in beacon HT/VHT/HE capabilities, but not security relevant — just PHY.

### Regulatory Domain

**Regdom:** Country code determines allowed channels, power, DFS, 6 GHz

- `iw reg get` shows regdom, e.g., `country US: DFS-FCC, (2402-2472 @ 40), (5170-5250 @ 80), (5250-5330 @ 80), (5490-5730 @ 160), (5735-5835 @ 80), (5925-6425 @ 160), (6425-6525 @ 20), (6525-6875 @ 160), (6875-7125 @ 160)`
- `iw reg set US` sets US, `iw reg set DE` sets Germany (EU)
- US allows Ch1-11 2.4 GHz, Ch36-165 5 GHz, Ch1-233 6 GHz with AFC, EU allows Ch1-13 2.4 GHz, Ch36-140 5 GHz, Ch1-233 6 GHz low power indoor only
- **For PT:** Check regdom — if regdom JP, Ch14 allowed (2484 MHz, 11b only), if US, Ch12-13 not allowed with 20 dBm, may need to set regdom to scan all channels. Evidence: Regdom from `iw reg get`.

## VAPT Relevance

- **Recon:** Scan all bands — 2.4 GHz Ch1-13, 5 GHz 36-165, 6 GHz 1-233 — `iw dev wlan0 scan` or Kismet or airodump-ng with channel hopping, or PcapInspector filter `wlan_mgt.ds.current_channel`
- **Enum:** Beacon shows channel, band, width, PHY capabilities (HT 20/40, VHT 80/160, HE 20/40/80/160), MCS, spatial streams, but security is RSN IE, WPS IE, PMF
- **Evidence:** Channel, band, width, PHY capabilities, MCS, spatial streams, vendor, signal, beacon interval, regdom

- `iw reg get` regdom, allowed channels, power

## Evidence Collection

- Beacon: Channel, band, width, PHY capabilities (HT 20/40, VHT 80/160, HE 20/40/80/160), MCS, spatial streams, vendor, signal, beacon interval, regdom
- Example: `Beacon SSID LAB-WIFI BSSID AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF Ch6 2.4 GHz 20 MHz HT 20 MHz only MCS 0-7 1 stream WPA2-PSK CCMP -45 dBm`

## Attack → Defense → Retest

- **Attack:** Observe 2.4 GHz AP with HT 40 MHz enabled, causes overlap with Ch1 and Ch11, interference
- **Defense:** Use 20 MHz only in 2.4 GHz — `ht_capab=[HT20]`, use 5/6 GHz for 40/80/160 MHz, check regdom, use DFS properly
- **Retest:** New beacon, verify HT capabilities show 20 MHz only, no 40 MHz, `wlan_mgt.ht.capabilities` shows 20 MHz, interference reduced

## Interactive Check

> Beacon shows `Primary Channel 6, Secondary Channel Offset Above, STA Channel Width Any, RIFS Mode, HT Protection Non-HT mixed, Non-Greenfield STAs present, OBSS Non-HT STAs present, Channel Center Frequency Segment 2 0, Dual Beacon, Dual CTS Protection, STBC Beacon, L-SIG TXOP Protection Full Support, PCO Active, PCO Phase`. What does it mean?

Answer: HT Operation IE — Primary Channel 6, Secondary Channel Offset Above (HT40+ uses Ch6+Ch10), STA Channel Width Any (20/40 allowed), RIFS Mode, HT Protection Non-HT mixed (legacy clients present), Non-Greenfield STAs present, OBSS Non-HT STAs present (overlapping BSS with legacy), etc. If 2.4 GHz with HT40+, Medium finding bad practice, should be 20 MHz only. Defense: `ht_capab=[HT20]` for 2.4 GHz. Evidence: Beacon HT Operation IE, channel.
