# Methodology & Ethics — Professional Wireless PT

## Learning Objectives
- Understand full VAPT methodology for wireless: Pre-engagement → Reporting → Retest
- Learn ROE, scope, authorization, legal boundaries — FCC, CFAA, GDPR, company policy
- Understand evidence chain of custody: hashes, frame numbers, reproducible commands
- Learn reporting structure: Title, Severity, Description, Evidence, Impact, Recommendation, Retest
- Understand difference between pentest, red team, bug bounty, research — scope matters
- Learn safe lab design: own infrastructure, Docker, Scapy PCAPs, no public Wi-Fi attacks

## Theory

### Why Methodology Matters

Running `airodump-ng` and `aireplay-ng --deauth` without methodology is not pentesting — it's just running commands. Professional wireless PT follows structured methodology to ensure coverage, evidence, impact assessment, remediation, retest, reporting.

**Without methodology:**
- Miss APs (only scan 2.4 GHz, miss 5/6 GHz)
- Miss clients (only beacons, not probes)
- Miss hidden SSIDs
- Miss enterprise misconfigs (PEAP no cert)
- No evidence — "WPS enabled" without frame number, config snippet
- No impact — technical only, not business risk
- No remediation — "fix it" not actionable
- No retest — fix not verified

**With methodology:**
- Systematic recon all bands, all channels
- Per SSID enumeration (RSN IE, WPS IE, PMF, AKM, ciphers, HT/VHT/HE, vendor)
- Per client enumeration (PNL, probe, assoc, EAP identity)
- Vuln analysis with severity based on impact + likelihood
- Evidence with hashes, frame numbers, filters, config snippets, reproducible
- Impact with business risk: network access, credential capture, lateral movement, segmentation bypass, data breach, compliance
- Recommendation actionable with config snippets: `wps_state=0`, `ieee80211w=2`, strong PSK 20+, `ca_cert` + `subject_match`, strong RADIUS secret 22+, `ap_isolate=1`, VLAN ACL deny
- Retest with new evidence
- Report professional

### Full Wireless PT Methodology

```
Pre-Engagement
  │
  ├─→ Define scope: SSIDs in scope, BSSIDs, physical locations (building, floors, parking lot), bands (2.4/5/6), channels, time window, ROE for deauth (usually not allowed in prod, only lab), no public Wi-Fi
  ├─→ Get authorization: Written permission, contact, emergency contact, scope document signed, legal approval
  ├─→ Understand environment: Number of APs, controller vs autonomous, Enterprise vs Personal, Guest/IoT/BYOD, VLANs, RADIUS, WIDS, monitoring
  └─→ Safety: Own infrastructure, Docker, Scapy PCAPs, lab SSIDs like LAB-WIFI, not Corp-WLAN prod

Reconnaissance (Passive, 0 RF tx, undetectable, safe)
  │
  ├─→ Scan all bands: 2.4 GHz Ch1-13, 5 GHz UNII-1/2/2e/3 36-165, 6 GHz U-NII-5-8 1-233
  ├─→ Capture beacons: SSID, BSSID, channel, band, width, security, RSN IE, WPS IE, HT/VHT/HE, vendor OUI, signal strength, beacon interval
  ├─→ Capture probe requests: Client MAC, PNL (Preferred Network List), vendor, signal, random MAC vs real MAC
  ├─→ Capture probe responses: Hidden SSID reveal, BSSID, channel, security
  ├─→ Capture auth/assoc: Client MAC, BSSID, reason codes, status codes
  ├─→ Capture EAPOL: Handshake M1-M4, PMKID, EAP type, identity, ANonce, SNonce, MIC
  ├─→ Capture EAP: PEAP, EAP-TLS, MSCHAPv2 challenge/response
  ├─→ Capture RADIUS: Access-Request/Accept/Reject, VLAN, secret strength (from config, not RF)
  ├─→ Tools: Kismet, airodump-ng, Wireshark, tshark, Scapy, PcapInspector
  └─→ Evidence: PCAP hash SHA256, frame numbers, filters, BSSID, SSID, channel, client MAC, EAP type

Enumeration & Vulnerability Analysis (Per SSID, per client, per config)
  │
  ├─→ Per SSID: RSN IE (group cipher, pairwise cipher, AKM, MFPC/MFPR), WPS IE (OUI 00:50:F2:04), HT/VHT/HE capabilities, PMF, channel width, vendor, signal, beacon interval, hidden?
  ├─→ Per client: PNL, probe, assoc, EAP identity, signal, vendor, random MAC?
  ├─→ Config audit: hostapd.conf (wps_state, ieee80211w, wpa_key_mgmt, rsn_pairwise, wpa_passphrase strength, ht_capab, ap_isolate), wpa_supplicant.conf (ca_cert, subject_match), FreeRADIUS clients.conf (ipaddr, secret), users (password strength, VLAN)
  ├─→ Vuln ID: WPS enabled High 11k, PMF disabled High deauth, weak PSK High offline audit, WPA3 transition same password PMF optional Medium downgrade, PEAP no ca_cert High credential capture, RADIUS weak secret Medium, Guest ap_isolate=0 Medium client attacks, captive MAC bypass Medium, VLAN ACL Corp→Guest High segmentation bypass, rogue not in authorized list High
  ├─→ Severity: Based on impact (network access, credential capture, lateral movement, data breach) + likelihood (exploitability, prerequisites), not just technical — business risk
  └─→ Evidence: Config snippets with line numbers, PCAP frame numbers, BSSID, channel, client MAC, EAP type, reason codes, hash

Exploitation / Validation (Simulated, authorized lab only, no public Wi-Fi, no prod deauth without explicit ROE)
  │
  ├─→ WEP: PTW 40k frames, key recovery in seconds (lab only, WEP is Critical)
  ├─→ WPA2-PSK weak: Handshake capture + offline audit with authorized wordlist (rockyou.txt) + hashcat 22000, or PMKID clientless
  ├─→ WPS: wash enumeration, reaver/bully PIN brute-force 11k max (lab only, rate limiting, lockout)
  ├─→ WPA3 transition downgrade: Force WPA2 via deauth if PMF optional, capture handshake, offline audit if PSK weak (lab only)
  ├─→ Deauth/Disassoc: Count deauth frames, reason codes, check PMF disabled, DoS impact, handshake capture facilitation (lab only, prod deauth usually not allowed)
  ├─→ Rogue AP: Same SSID different BSSID not in authorized list, different channel/vendor, client assoc to rogue, Evil Twin PSK mode if PSK known, Enterprise rogue RADIUS captures MSCHAPv2 challenge/response hashcat 5500 (lab only)
  ├─→ Captive Portal: Open, HTTP redirect, login POST over HTTP, MAC-based session bypass via sniffing MAC (open no encryption) and spoofing (lab only)
  ├─→ Enterprise: PEAP without ca_cert → rogue RADIUS captures MSCHAPv2, EAP-TLS mutual cert most secure, RADIUS weak secret brute-force, users weak password
  ├─→ Corporate: Rogue + segmentation bypass + isolation bypass chain
  ├─→ Tools: aircrack-ng, hashcat, hcxpcapngtool, wash, reaver, hostapd, wpa_supplicant, FreeRADIUS, Scapy, PcapInspector, ConfigViewer, AttackDefenseRetest
  └─→ Evidence: New PCAP with deauth frames, rogue BSSID, client assoc to rogue, handshake, PMKID, challenge/response, hashcat output (authorized), config, logs

Post-Exploitation (After network access, authorized only)
  │
  ├─→ Internal scanning: nmap, VLAN hopping if ACL misconfig, check if Corp VLAN 100 can ping Guest VLAN 200
  ├─→ Client attacks: ARP spoof if isolation disabled, sniff, MitM, LLMNR/NetBIOS/mDNS poisoning
  ├─→ Credential capture: EAP creds, internal creds, hash capture
  ├─→ Data breach: Access to internal resources, file shares, databases, compliance violation (GDPR, PCI, HIPAA)
  └─→ Evidence: Ping results, nmap, ARP spoof, creds (redacted), logs

Impact Assessment
  │
  ├─→ Technical: What can attacker do? Network access, decrypt traffic, inject, DoS, credential capture, lateral movement, segmentation bypass, client attacks
  ├─→ Business: What is business risk? Data breach, compliance violation, financial loss, reputation, IP theft, regulatory fines
  ├─→ Likelihood: How easy to exploit? Prerequisites? WPS 11k easy, weak PSK depends on wordlist, PEAP no cert needs client without validation, rogue needs proximity
  ├─→ Severity: Critical (WEP, segmentation to sensitive VLAN), High (WPS, weak PSK, PEAP no cert, rogue, segmentation bypass), Medium (PMF disabled, open no isolation, captive MAC bypass, RADIUS weak), Low (2.4 GHz 40 MHz bad practice, DFS)
  └─→ Overall risk: If multiple High, overall High, if Critical, overall Critical

Remediation (Actionable, with config snippets)
  │
  ├─→ WEP: Migrate to WPA3 or WPA2 CCMP, PMF required, strong PSK
  ├─→ WPA2-PSK weak: Strong PSK 20+ chars random, not in wordlists, unique per SSID, WPA3-only where possible, PMF required
  ├─→ WPS: Disable WPS wps_state=0, no PBC via UPnP, strong lockout if needed but better disabled
  ├─→ PMF disabled: Enable PMF required ieee80211w=2, WPA3-only mandates PMF, WIDS detect deauth flood, SA Query
  ├─→ WPA3 transition: Prefer WPA3-only, ieee80211w=2, strong PSK 20+ random, monitor for downgrade
  ├─→ PEAP no ca_cert: Enforce ca_cert + subject_match/altsubject_match/domain_suffix_match via MDM/GPO, prefer EAP-TLS mutual cert, strong RADIUS secret 22+, RadSec TLS, PMF required, WIDS
  ├─→ RADIUS weak: Strong secret 22+ chars random per NAS, RadSec TLS, strong user passwords complexity lockout 2FA, EAP-TLS, restrict clients.conf to AP IPs not 0.0.0.0/0, monitor logs SIEM, regular audit users, PMF, WPA3-Enterprise
  ├─→ Guest isolation: ap_isolate=1, private VLANs, firewall rules
  ├─→ Captive portal: HTTPS portal, MAC+cookie+IP+token+timeout+re-auth, OWE or WPA2-PSK for guest encryption, WIDS rogue portal detection
  ├─→ VLAN ACL: Deny inter-VLAN, private VLANs, firewall rules, deny Corp→Guest and Guest→Corp, allow only required
  ├─→ Rogue: WIDS authorized AP list BSSID channel vendor signal, alert, contain, 802.1X cert validation, PMF required, strong PSK, WPA3, client cert
  └─→ Quick wins vs long-term: Quick wins disable WPS, strong PSK, PMF required, ca_cert, strong RADIUS secret, ap_isolate=1, VLAN ACL deny; Long-term WPA3-only, EAP-TLS, RadSec, WIDS, monitoring SIEM, training, audits, 6 GHz, OWE

Retest (Verify fix with new evidence)
  │
  ├─→ New PCAP: Verify beacon no WPS IE (filter wps no results), RSN MFPR=1 PMF required, 20 MHz only, WPA3-only AKM SAE only
  ├─→ Config audit: Verify wps_state=0, ieee80211w=2, strong PSK not in wordlist, ca_cert present, strong secret 22+, ap_isolate=1, VLAN ACL deny
  ├─→ Offline audit: Authorized wordlist should fail, handshake audit fails with strong PSK
  ├─→ Rogue detection: WIDS no rogue, client not assoc to rogue, 802.1X cert validation passes (client with ca_cert rejects rogue self-signed)
  ├─→ Segmentation: VLAN ping fails ACL deny, isolation enabled clients can't ping
  ├─→ Deauth: PMF required deauth spoof fails, SA Query works
  ├─→ Evidence: New PCAP hash, frame numbers, config hash, logs, what should happen vs what actually happened
  └─→ Verification: Reproducible, with hashes, frame numbers, filters, commands

Reporting (Professional VAPT structure)
  │
  ├─→ Executive Summary: Overall risk, key findings, business impact, quick wins, long-term, for management
  ├─→ Scope: SSIDs, BSSIDs, physical locations, bands, channels, time window, ROE, authorization, methodology
  ├─→ Methodology: Tools, recon, enum, vuln, exploitation (simulated), post-exploitation, evidence collection
  ├─→ Findings: Each finding with Title, Severity, Description, Technical Details, Affected Component (SSID, BSSID, channel, band, security, vendor, client MAC), Evidence (PCAP hash, frame numbers, filters, config snippet with line numbers, logs, screenshots, commands), Impact (technical + business), Recommendation (actionable config snippets), References (802.11 spec, OWASP, NIST), Retest (what should happen, what actually happened, new evidence)
  ├─→ Risk Summary: Overall risk, risk matrix, findings count by severity, quick wins vs long-term
  ├─→ Recommendation Summary: Prioritized, quick wins vs long-term, config snippets
  ├─→ Retest: Summary of retest results, fixes verified, residual risk
  ├─→ References: 802.11-2020, OWASP, NIST SP 800-153, hostapd docs, FreeRADIUS docs
  ├─→ Appendices: PCAPs, configs, logs, tools, commands, glossary
  └─→ Template: ReportEditor in this platform, export MD

```

### Legal & Ethics

**Authorization:**
- Written permission required for any wireless testing — even passive recon in some jurisdictions may be considered interception
- Scope document signed by asset owner, legal, security
- ROE: What is allowed? Deauth? Rogue AP? Usually not in prod, only lab
- Time window: When can you test? Avoid business hours for active tests
- Contact: Emergency contact, escalation path
- No public Wi-Fi: Don't test Starbucks, Airport, Hotel without explicit authorization — even if open, it's not yours

**Laws:**
- **FCC (US):** 2.4/5/6 GHz unlicensed, but power limits, DFS, jamming illegal (Wi-Fi jammer illegal)
- **CFAA (US):** Unauthorized access to computer network — wireless network access without authorization may violate
- **GDPR (EU):** Capturing client MAC, PNL, traffic may be personal data — need authorization, data minimization, retention policy
- **Company policy:** Even if you work for company, need authorization to test Corp-WLAN — don't test without written permission

**Ethics:**
- Don't capture sensitive data (passwords, personal data) beyond what needed for evidence, redact in report
- Don't disrupt service — deauth flood DoS is disruption, only in lab with explicit ROE
- Don't use findings for personal gain — report, remediate, retest
- Responsible disclosure — if you find rogue AP, report to security, don't exploit
- Privacy — probe requests leak PNL, but don't use for tracking, just for assessment

**Safe Lab Design:**
- Own infrastructure: Your own APs, your own clients, your own RADIUS, Docker, Scapy PCAPs
- Lab SSIDs: LAB-WIFI, HIDDEN-LAB, LAB-WPS, etc., not Corp-WLAN prod
- PCAPs: Self-generated via Scapy, like in this academy — zero-cost, local-first, reproducible
- No public Wi-Fi attacks: Don't test arbitrary public Wi-Fi — use lab
- This academy: All PCAPs self-generated, configs self-generated, simulated labs — safe, zero-cost, local-first

### Evidence Chain of Custody

**Why chain of custody?**
- Evidence must be reproducible — another tester should be able to reproduce with same PCAP, config, commands
- Evidence must have integrity — hash proves file not tampered
- Evidence must have context — frame numbers, filters, BSSID, channel, time

**Checklist:**
- PCAP file: `sha256sum capture.pcapng` → hash, file size, creation time, tool used (Scapy, Kismet, airodump-ng)
- Frame numbers: Beacon f1, Probe Req f3, Probe Resp f10, Auth f5-6, Assoc f7-8, EAPOL f9-12, Deauth f1-12
- Filters: `wlan.fc.type_subtype==8`, `eapol`, `wlan.bssid==AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF`, `wps`, `wlan_mgt.ssid==LAB-WIFI`
- BSSID, SSID, channel, band, width, security, vendor, signal, beacon interval
- Client MAC, PNL, EAP identity, signal
- Config: `hostapd.conf` hash, line numbers, relevant lines
- Logs: RADIUS logs, hostapd logs, wpa_supplicant logs, with timestamps
- Screenshots: Wireshark, airodump-ng, Kismet, with annotations
- Commands: Exact commands executed, with output, with timestamps
- What should happen vs what actually happened — for retest

### Reporting Structure (Professional)

```
Title: Insecure Wireless Configuration — WPS Enabled + PMF Disabled
Severity: Medium (based on impact + likelihood, business risk)
Description: The wireless network LAB-WIFI (BSSID AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF, Channel 6, 2.4 GHz, WPA2-PSK CCMP) is configured with WPS enabled and PMF disabled. WPS introduces PIN brute-force risk (11k max), PMF disabled allows deauthentication spoofing.
Technical Details: WPS PIN 8-digit with halves flaw: first half 10^4 + second half 10^3 = 11k max, not 10^8. WPS IE 221 OUI 00:50:F2:04 present in beacon. PMF (802.11w) disabled, management frames unauthenticated, attacker can spoof deauth. HT 40MHz in 2.4GHz bad practice.
Affected Component: SSID LAB-WIFI, BSSID AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF, Channel 6, Band 2.4 GHz, Width 20 MHz, Security WPA2-PSK CCMP, Vendor Cisco (OUI 00:11:22), Signal -45 dBm
Evidence: PCAP beacon-only.pcapng SHA256 abc123 Frame 1 Beacon SSID LAB-WIFI BSSID AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF Ch6 WPS IE OUI 00:50:F2:04 present, Config hostapd.conf SHA256 def456 wps_state=2 line 10, ieee80211w=0 line 11, ht_capab [HT40+] line 12, Wireshark filters wlan.fc.type_subtype==8 shows 2 beacons, wps filter shows WPS IE, wlan_mgt.rsn.capabilities.mfpr==0 shows PMF not required
Impact: Technical: WPS PIN brute-force → PSK recovery → network access, PMF disabled → DoS via deauth flood, handshake capture facilitation, Evil Twin facilitation, 40MHz → interference. Business: Network access, lateral movement, data breach, compliance violation, reputation, financial loss. Likelihood: High if WPS enabled and no rate limiting, Medium if PMF disabled but requires proximity. Severity: Medium, but if WPS + weak PSK + PMF disabled combined, High.
Recommendation: Disable WPS: wps_state=0, no PBC via UPnP. Enable PMF required: ieee80211w=2. Use 20MHz only in 2.4GHz: ht_capab=[HT20]. Strong PSK 20+ chars random, not in wordlists, unique per SSID. Consider WPA3-only with SAE and PMF required. Quick win: wps_state=0, ieee80211w=2, ht_capab=[HT20]. Long-term: WPA3-only, WIDS, monitoring.
References: IEEE 802.11-2020, OWASP Wireless Security Testing Guide, NIST SP 800-153, hostapd documentation, WPS flaws (Bongard 2014)
Retest: After fix: Verify beacon no WPS IE (filter wps no results), verify RSN capabilities MFPR=1 PMF required, verify 20MHz only (HT capabilities), verify offline audit with authorized wordlist fails, verify deauth spoof fails (hardware lab, SA Query). New evidence: PCAP beacon-only-fixed.pcapng SHA256 xyz789 Frame 1 Beacon SSID LAB-WIFI BSSID AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF Ch6 WPA2-PSK CCMP no WPS IE, RSN MFPR=1, HT 20MHz only, Config hostapd.conf fixed wps_state=0 ieee80211w=2 ht_capab=[HT20], offline audit with rockyou.txt fails, deauth spoof fails (lab).
```

**Key:** Evidence with frame numbers, hashes, filters, config line numbers — not just "WPS enabled". Recommendation actionable with config snippets — not just "fix it". Retest with new evidence — what should happen vs what actually happened.

### Pentest vs Red Team vs Bug Bounty vs Research

- **Pentest:** Time-boxed, scope defined, ROE, report, retest — this academy teaches pentest methodology
- **Red Team:** Adversary simulation, broader scope, stealth, persistence, lateral movement, physical, wireless is one vector
- **Bug Bounty:** Scope via program, rewards, usually web/API, wireless rarely in scope unless explicitly
- **Research:** New vulnerabilities, protocol flaws, tool development — advanced, after mastering PT

**For this academy:** Focus on pentest methodology, professional reporting, evidence, remediation, retest. Red team and research later.

## VAPT Relevance

- **Pre-engagement:** Most important — define scope, get authorization, understand environment, safety, legal
- **Evidence:** Chain of custody — hashes, frame numbers, filters, reproducible
- **Reporting:** Professional structure — Title, Severity, Description, Evidence, Impact, Recommendation, Retest
- **Ethics:** Don't test public Wi-Fi, own infrastructure, authorized labs only, privacy, no disruption

## Common Mistakes

- No written authorization — test Corp-WLAN without permission, violate CFAA, company policy
- No scope — scan all channels, but miss 6 GHz, or scan public Wi-Fi without authorization
- No evidence — "WPS enabled" without frame number, config snippet, hash
- No impact — technical only, not business risk
- No remediation — "fix it" not actionable
- No retest — fix not verified
- Disruption — deauth flood in prod, DoS, without ROE
- Privacy violation — capture personal data, PNL, traffic, without authorization, data minimization

## Attack → Defense → Retest (Methodology)

- **Attack:** No methodology, miss APs, no evidence, no impact, no remediation, no retest
- **Defense:** Follow methodology Pre-engagement→Recon→Enum→Vuln→Exploit→Post-Exploit→Impact→Remediation→Retest→Reporting, evidence chain custody, professional reporting
- **Retest:** Verify methodology followed, evidence reproducible, fixes verified

## Interactive Check
