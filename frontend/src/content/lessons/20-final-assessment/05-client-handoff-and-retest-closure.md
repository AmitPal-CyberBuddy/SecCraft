# Hand Off a Clear Report and an Honest Retest Plan

> **Available now: professional communication practice.** WF-REVIEW-07 provides a report template and review criteria, not a submission service or live assessment. Execution and remediation effectiveness remain **NOT TESTED**.

## Lead with the decision the owner can make

Use the [WF-REVIEW-07 ZIP](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07.zip) and [client handoff template](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/client-handoff.md). Write a summary of at most 150 words: scope and evidence mode, supported result, unresolved questions, first action/owner and limits. Avoid “the network is secure” or “retest passed” when the evidence is three staged beacons.

A useful summary need not list every frame. Keep the underlying [worksheet](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/worksheet.md) traceable: original hash, file/frame/field, interpretation, alternative, impact assumption and next authorized test. Preserve non-findings and exclusions rather than burying them in a disclaimer.

## Close only the question that was checked

CR-20 has multiple possible closure questions. Does OPS-A advertise the intended policy in the staged file? Can approved clients negotiate it? Are disallowed modes rejected? Was the old passphrase retired on all relevant assets? Does the unlisted BSSID belong to this owner? A supplied beacon can answer only the advertisement question.

Propose, but do not execute, a separate authorized retest with exact client/AP identities, allowed techniques, window/budget, negotiated AKM/PMF and AP/client logs, positive controls, expected rejection behavior, stop conditions and approved rollback. Correlate evidence before claiming causation. Document which closure conditions remain open and who must supply the missing evidence. No live configuration, reset or cleanup occurred in this exercise.

## Protect the evidence you hand over

The current pack is fictional and may be kept locally for practice. Future real captures can expose identities, addresses and credentials. Agree named recipients, encrypted delivery, access expiry, retention and backup/deletion procedures with the owner before collecting. Keep originals read-only; label redacted/derived copies and preserve traceability. A hash cannot certify lawful collection, custody or authenticity by itself.

Do not upload real captures or secrets through public issues or the platform feedback form. The downloaded worksheet is not submitted to a grader; local files and any peer copies remain your responsibility. Browser completion cannot verify that copies were deleted or a reviewer approved the work.

## End with an explicit readiness boundary

Use the [review guide](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/review-guide.md) to identify a concrete next learning step. You can now practice source-cited reporting and explain missing evidence. Field competence still requires an approved isolated environment, actual execution, reliable reset, independent review and a validated assessment process. C-20 remains an evidence practicum; Northwind remains planning-only. Finishing the path does not change those facts.
