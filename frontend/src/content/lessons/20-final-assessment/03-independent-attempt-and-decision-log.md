# Make an Independent Attempt and Preserve Your Decisions

> **Available now: offline evidence practice.** WF-REVIEW-07 extends the existing public C-20 case. It is not unseen exam content, a live engagement or independently graded certification. Hosted execution remains unavailable; live outcomes are **NOT TESTED**.

## Begin with permission, not an attack list

Download the [WF-REVIEW-07 ZIP](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07.zip), read its [scope](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/scope.md) and [C-20 case notes](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/CASE_NOTES.md), then start an [attempt worksheet](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/worksheet.md). These files authorize offline interpretation only. Northwind ENG-01 still lacks execution evidence and is a separate planning exercise. Do not join earlier course scenes into a fictional collected engagement.

Before looking at reference answers, save your scope, inventory and first decisions. Disclose prior exposure to C-20: independence means doing and explaining your own work, not pretending public answers were hidden. A suggested 80-minute budget helps prioritize; it is not a validated professional exam limit. If time expires, submit unresolved questions rather than guesses.

## Inventory before severity

Use the [baseline JSON](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/capstone-baseline.json) and [staged retest JSON](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/capstone-retest.json), or independently decode the captures in the ZIP. JSON is a generated view of the same bytes, not a second witness. Cite filename, current hash, frame, field and actual review method. The [manifest](/wireless-practice/WF-REVIEW-07/SHA256SUMS) establishes byte consistency, not genuine radio collection.

Reconcile all three BSSIDs with the partial owner inventory. Prioritize the intended CR-20 change and unresolved ownership, not whichever network name sounds important. Is the owner assertion sufficient? What can a beacon establish? What evidence is needed before an ownership hypothesis becomes a finding?

Record each decision as question, priority, permitted action, evidence, conclusion and next authorized test. Include one alternative that would overturn your conclusion. A missing inventory row, identical SSID or locally administered address does not establish malicious ownership.

## Choose and disclose your method

Browser-only learners can review supplied JSON and documents and label their work **supplied-export review**. Packet-tool learners can read the original PCAPNG locally and record the actual tool version, command/filter and output. Neither route transmits traffic. Published fixture credentials are for known-answer packet checks, not proof that you recovered a client's password.

Do not claim to run tools you did not run. Do not treat the browser command simulator as a terminal. Preserve the initial worksheet before consulting the public guide; later corrections belong in a change log. Local lesson completion records participation, not verification of the attempt.
