## Threat boundary

An engagement claim must tie an owned app, a particular installed build and a controlled observation to one trust boundary. Independent practice should also discard unsupported hypotheses; broad tool coverage without evidence is not an assessment.

## Worked observation → interpretation → limit

The Notes Boundary source offers a plausible mock-account ownership issue in its vulnerable flavor. The separate storage and WebView excerpts are independent fictional files. Joining them into a chain or calling them findings in the demo would fabricate evidence. The fixed flavor is a planned comparison, not a completed retest unless actually executed.

The relevant source excerpt is labeled **Case dossier (combined authored excerpts)**. In the Lab tab, read the candidate and safer comparison before answering. ## A safer decision

Self-review and quizzes cannot certify Android engagement or interview proficiency. Production reports, client sign-off, supervised work and a verified device lab are outside this teaching pack. A finding requires an exact artifact hash, OS/API and target SDK context where relevant, a controlled positive and negative observation, and a bounded conclusion. In this authored example, the only safe claim is what the named source files express. The distinct Android cases are *not* one deployed application.
