# Wireless learner pilot — ready to recruit, not yet run

2026-10-01. Released baseline: 15 modules, seven phases, 53 lessons; estimated 73 hours. Those hours are author estimates, not measured learner completion times. This plan prepares a formative teaching pilot, not certification validation.

## Questions to answer

1. Can a novice find and finish browser-only evidence practice without mistaking the simulator for a shell?
2. Can learners distinguish advertised policy, negotiated state, applied authorization and business impact?
3. Do the shorter independent checkpoints add transfer practice, or merely repeat their preceding lesson?
4. Does the revised WPA2/Enterprise order reduce backtracking and certificate/AAA confusion?
5. Are modern-feature recognition sections helpful, or distracting before core competencies are established?
6. Can learners deliver a concise supported finding, narrow no-finding and an unresolved question without inventing a live outcome?

## Participants and consent

Proposed first round: six voluntary adult learners (two networking novices, two comfortable with captures, two with prior assessment experience), plus one independent wireless instructor and one protocol reviewer. These are recruitment targets, not participants already enrolled. Record prior C-20/answer exposure and optional tool availability; do not demand personal identity documents or production access.

Use synthetic course files only. No RF equipment, real credentials, client captures, external network tests or sensitive employer data. Ask permission for observation; screen recording is optional and separately consented. Allow withdrawal without penalty. Do not use platform XP as a competence score.

A coordinator must agree the owner, reviewers, dates, consent form, access controls and retention before collecting any pilot data. Proposed handling: random participant IDs, separate consent records, encrypted restricted notes, aggregate findings, raw notes deleted 30 days after the review decision unless a different consented policy is approved. No public feedback form uploads of raw recordings or evidence. No collection begins merely because this document exists.

## Session design

Two moderated sessions of at most 90 minutes per participant, with breaks and permission to stop. Use selected tasks rather than promising to validate a 73-hour path in three hours. Pilot both browser-only and optional local-tool routes; failed local installation must not block the browser route.

| Task | Suggested time | Evidence of learning | Instructor observes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations: WF-FND-01 inventory and one bounded claim | 20 min | cited BSSID/frame, hidden-name limit, unresolved ownership | vocabulary, artifact access, provenance |
| WPA2: first-station worked example then other-station checkpoint | 20 min | message classification and partial-exchange limit | whether checkpoint transfers rather than repeats |
| Candidate audit or supplied-result route | 10 min | tested-set size, match/non-recovery limit, actual method label | installation friction and false tool-execution claims |
| Enterprise: same-CA wrong-name certificate decision | 20 min | distinguishes chain/name, file check and client behavior | prerequisite order and trust-model confusion |
| Boundary model: F4/F7/F8 | 15 min | deviation, uncertainty and stop-scope remain distinct | guessing, missing positive controls, claim inflation |
| C-20 handoff: observation, non-finding, unknown | 25 min | cited claim ledger and <=150-word summary | reference-answer exposure and unsupported closure |
| Targeted reread/compare + debrief | remaining time | marked confusing/redundant passages | where a merge would remove repetition without removing a skill |

Give only navigation help before the first attempt. Record assistance explicitly; then allow the public guide and save corrections as a second version. Do not call the second version an unseen result. Missing evidence is a valid conclusion, not a failure to complete an attack.

Include phone-width reading (320–390px), desktop, keyboard-only interaction and at least one learner who normally uses assistive technology if a willing participant is available. Automated axe checks are not a substitute for that person's experience; do not claim accessibility-user testing until it happens.

## Decision rules agreed before the pilot

These are formative review triggers, not statistically validated pass thresholds:

- Any reproducible unsafe instruction, false credential/enforcement claim, broken required download or misleading completion label blocks pilot expansion until corrected and retested.
- If two or more learners independently fail at the same prerequisite or wording, revise that material and repeat the task with a fresh attempt or new participant.
- Retain a separate checkpoint only if it elicits a distinct decision or reveals a misconception missed by the guided lesson. Otherwise merge its exercise into the core lesson in a separately tested progress/deep-link migration.
- Instructor and protocol reviewer reconcile disagreements against actual files and primary references. Missing evidence stays unresolved; do not force consensus by marking the intended answer correct.
- Report route-specific completion/assistance times and ranges; six participants do not justify broad efficacy or difficulty-equivalence claims.
- Re-estimate lesson/path hours only after measuring reading, exercises and setup separately. Do not count optional external reading as completed core practice.

## Merge candidates to test

- WPA2 four-way lesson + decision checkpoint: the duplicate walkthrough has already moved into the core lesson. Compare whether the independent second-station attempt still merits a separate stop.
- Rogue-infrastructure lesson + look-alike checkpoint: repeated frame narration removed; evaluate cross-case transfer before collapsing the checkpoint.
- Enterprise test-plan lesson + decision checkpoint: collection guidance consolidated; evaluate whether the final cross-boundary review identifies errors after the reordered certificate/policy material.
- Certificate-file verification + optional owned-client procedure: **do not merge merely because both mention certificates**. Their execution modes and evidence outcomes differ.
- WEP + WPA2 or portals + segmentation: **do not merge whole modules**. Different mechanisms, prerequisites and assessment questions would become harder to distinguish.

## Exit deliverables and current status

Deliver anonymized issue log, corrected passages, before/after attempt examples with consent, reviewer decisions, measured timings and a justified keep/merge decision per candidate. Any structural merge needs old-link redirects, retained historical completion, no duplicate XP, explicit new-content credit policy and migration tests; do not silently grant a broader outcome from an old narrow completion.

**Current status:** plan and templates prepared. No recruitment, consent, learner session, instructor approval, human accessibility assessment or scoring-equivalence study has been performed. Full live Enterprise/boundary execution gates remain open and are outside this offline pilot.
